# PocketChores

**Chores and pocket money, sorted**

> #28 of 33 in the build order · Harder · Revenue potential: Medium · Family, Pets & Plants

## What it is

Parents set chores and rewards, kids tick them off, and PocketChores keeps the pocket-money tally and each child's savings goals. Starts as a single family device; separate kid devices come later.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Weekly pocket-money day is a strong built-in cue, parents pay for family organisation, and it teaches kids about money (pairs with MoneyMinute and StackUp).

## Who it's for

Parents of children aged roughly 5–14.

**The problem:** Chore charts on the fridge get ignored, and pocket-money maths causes arguments.

## Version one

- Chores with points or money values
- Kids tick off; a parent approves
- Weekly pocket-money total
- Kids' savings goals
- Family device mode (no child accounts)

**Example:** On Saturday a parent approves the week's ticked chores and sees each child's pocket money and savings progress.

## Later

- Separate kid devices (sync)
- Rewards shop
- Allowance history
